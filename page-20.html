Beyond our worlds

Which world is most amazing? There is no single answer. Beyond Neptune lies the observed Kuiper Belt, and much farther away scientists infer a vast Oort Cloud. Our whole solar system travels inside the Milky Way — with more questions waiting ahead.

‹›
Observed: Many icy worlds circle beyond Neptune in the Kuiper Belt.
Inferred: Comet paths point to a distant Oort Cloud, but no spacecraft has seen it.
A galactic year: The Sun takes roughly 230 million years to circle the Milky Way.
