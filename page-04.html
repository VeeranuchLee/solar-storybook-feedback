Mercury

The speedy little planet

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun—and the fastest planet in its orbit. It races around the Sun, but turns very slowly.

Ari and Dot flew past its ancient craters. The sunny side was extremely hot, while the dark side became extremely cold.

Mercury’s craters

Mercury’s old surface is covered with impact craters. With almost no atmosphere, incoming rocks do not burn up first.
